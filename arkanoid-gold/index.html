HOW TO PLAY
Pick your bet, then BUY A BALL. It bounces on its own and speeds up with every bounce. If it falls into an OUT corner, it's gone. Your bet locks once you buy — RESET to change it.
A brick's label is its payout multiplier. Each hit on a payout brick adds a small trickle (BET × 0.03); the hit that breaks it pays the full multiplier instead — a 2x brick pays BET × 2. The corner badge shows hits left to break.
METAL bricks never break and pay nothing, but each hit has a 10% chance to drop a 0.1x coin.
★ PIÑATA bricks burst into 8–16 coins when broken.
Catch a falling 0.1x coin with a ball to collect BET × 0.1.
Some bricks slide (↔). Clear every breakable brick to finish the level — your live balls carry into the next one.
Each ball you buy costs more than the last. Use 1× / 2× / 4× to set game speed.
